Use this checklist during a drawing review or before a site briefing. Adapt it to the project's document control process.

- Confirm the drawing number, revision, status and issue date.
- Compare the drawing with the current drawing register.
- Check relevant plans, sections, details and specifications together.
- Identify dimensions and levels that need confirmation.
- Review interfaces with structure, services, finishes and access.
- Record unresolved questions with their exact drawing reference.
- Agree who will raise each query and where the response will be filed.

For a fuller explanation, read [our guide to reading construction drawings](/en/insights/reading-construction-drawings/).
