An early warning is a way for the project team to identify and manage a potential problem while there is still time to act. This illustrated guide follows a site team through a possible utility diversion delay and the steps they take to discuss it.

Inside the free guide:

- When the Contractor or Project Manager should consider giving an early warning.
- The purpose of the Early Warning Register and Early Warning Meetings.
- A practical example of discussing mitigation and keeping a record of agreed actions.
- The difference between the early warning process and the compensation event process.
- A short notification template and summary checklist.

The guide discusses NEC4 ECC in general terms. Check the signed contract, its amendments and the project facts before relying on any clause or time requirement in practice.
