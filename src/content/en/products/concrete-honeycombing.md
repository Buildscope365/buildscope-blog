Concrete honeycombing needs more than a quick surface patch. This short comic follows Sam and Alex as they examine the defect, consider how it happened and decide what to record before repair.

Inside the free guide:

- Common causes, including poor compaction, congested reinforcement and placement difficulties.
- Questions that help distinguish a local surface defect from a case requiring further assessment.
- A practical sequence for documenting the location, agreeing a repair method and checking the result.
- A site inspection checklist and photo record pointers.

Use it as a discussion aid on site. The assessment and repair method for a real defect should follow the project specification and the responsible engineer's direction.
