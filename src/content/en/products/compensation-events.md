Extra work does not automatically become a compensation event. In this short illustrated guide, Alex and Sam work through the checks that matter before drawing a conclusion.

Inside the free guide:

- Check the Scope and the contractual event against the project facts.
- Compare three site situations: unexpected ground conditions, a Project Manager's design instruction and a Contractor's proposed method change.
- Follow a practical path from identifying an event to notification and assessment.
- Gather contract references, instructions, records, programme information and cost evidence.

This guide discusses NEC4 ECC in general terms. Check the signed contract, applicable options, amendments and project facts before applying any clause or time limit to a real project.
