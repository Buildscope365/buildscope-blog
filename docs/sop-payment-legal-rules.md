## Implementation boundaries

- Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` calendar values. Pure calculations use UTC **only as an ordinal day representation**; no instant is converted into a local date. Browser `asOf` is explicitly formatted in `Asia/Hong_Kong`.
- Statutory period rolls only endpoint under Cap. 1 s.71. A list of public holidays, gale and black rainstorm warning days is supplied by the user; Sunday is handled automatically. A statutory output without completed holiday verification is marked provisional, and overdue risk is suppressed.
- Contract input is either a fixed specified date or a simple integer calendar-day interval from service/billing date. Contractual dates and formulas are taken as user-verified. Ambiguous working-day, certification, month-end or conditional clauses require manual legal assessment.
- Where a late claim resets a billing-based contractual period, s.15(3)/20(3) substitutes the actual service date **for that formula**. The statutory period remains based on service under s.18. This avoids treating the CIC FAQ's short answer to question 24 as a retroactive service-date substitution.
- The screen is deliberately preliminary. It does not decide whether mixed work is qualifying, whether a document is a valid payment claim/response, an amount is admitted, a dispute exists, or adjudication is available.
- No form values are persisted or sent to the server or analytics. Printing uses the browser's print-to-PDF facility.
